Thank-you notes

A thank-you note is one of the shortest letters you'll write — and one of the most powerful. Use it after a medical appointment, a job interview, a gift, a favor, or anytime someone helped you.

When to use

  • After a doctor, nurse, or therapist appointment
  • After a job interview
  • After receiving a gift
  • After someone helped you with paperwork, a move, or a problem
  • To thank a teacher, coworker, or neighbor

Step-by-step

1. Keep it short

Three to five sentences is enough. You don't need to fill a page.

2. Name what you're thanking them for

Be specific: "Thank you for explaining my blood test results" lands better than "Thank you for everything."

3. Say why it mattered

"I felt much less worried after our talk" or "I used the gift right away."

4. Use the template

See ../templates/thank-you-template.md or templates/printable/printable-thank-you.html.

5. Draft with AI (optional)

Thank-you notes are personal, so you may not need AI. But if you're stuck, copy the prompt from AI prompt templates.

Thank-you note template

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS OR EMAIL]
[DATE]

Dear [NAME],

Thank you for [what they did]. [One sentence about why it helped or mattered to you.]

[Optional: one more sentence — how you'll use the gift, what happens next, or when you hope to see them again.]

With thanks,
[YOUR NAME]

AI prompt for thank-you notes

Please write a short, warm thank-you note. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [NAME]
- What they did: [DESCRIPTION]
- Why it helped or mattered: [ONE SENTENCE]
- Optional extra: [HOW YOU'LL USE THE GIFT / WHEN YOU'LL SEE THEM AGAIN]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]

Requirements:
- 3 to 5 sentences
- Tone: warm, sincere, brief
- No formal business language needed

Write only the note. No extra commentary.

Example (filled in)

Riley Chen
riley@email.com
April 10, 2026

Dear Dr. Bennett,

Thank you for seeing me on Tuesday and explaining my blood test results in plain language. I felt much less worried after our talk, and I know exactly what to work on before my next visit.

I appreciate your time.

With thanks,
Riley Chen
