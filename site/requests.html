Request letters

Use a request letter when you need something from someone: medical records, workplace accommodations, information, a meeting, or a document. The trick is to make it easy for the reader to say yes.

When to use

  • Requesting your medical records
  • Requesting workplace accommodations
  • Asking for a meeting or phone call
  • Requesting information from a company or agency
  • Asking for a copy of a letter, report, or decision

Step-by-step

1. Say exactly what you need

Vague requests get vague answers or no answer. "I want my records" is better than "I want stuff about my health."

2. Include what helps them find it

Dates, account references (last 4 digits only if unsure), names, and case numbers help the reader locate your file quickly.

3. Say how to send it and by when

Email address, mailing address, fax number — and a reasonable deadline (two weeks is standard for most requests).

4. Use the template

See ../templates/request-template.md or templates/printable/printable-request.html.

5. Draft with AI

Copy the request prompt from AI prompt templates, fill in your details, and paste it into your AI chat tool.

6. Review and send

Check that you didn't include anything from the "never include" list. Send and keep a copy.

Medical records reminder

If you're requesting your own medical records:

  • You have a right to see your own records in most cases (U.S. — HIPAA).
  • Include your full name, date of birth (records offices usually require it), and the dates of treatment.
  • This guide is education only — it is not medical or legal advice.

Request letter template

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS — optional for email]
[YOUR EMAIL OR PHONE]
[DATE]

[ORGANIZATION NAME]
[DEPARTMENT OR ADDRESS]

Re: Request for [WHAT YOU NEED]
[Account or reference number — last 4 digits only if unsure]

Dear [DEPARTMENT or Contact Name],

I am writing to request [exactly what you need].

Details to help you find it:
- [Detail 1 — dates, names, account reference]
- [Detail 2 — dates of treatment, dates of service]
- [Detail 3 — any reference numbers]

Please send the [documents/information] to:
- [EMAIL ADDRESS] or
- [MAILING ADDRESS]

If possible, I would appreciate receiving them by [DATE, about 2 weeks out].

Please contact me at [PHONE OR EMAIL] if you need anything else.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]

AI prompt for requests

Please write a clear, polite request letter. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [ORGANIZATION / DEPARTMENT]
- What I am requesting: [EXACTLY WHAT YOU NEED]
- Details to help them find it: [DATES, NAMES, REFERENCE — last 4 digits only]
- How to send it: [EMAIL OR MAILING ADDRESS]
- Deadline: [DATE, about 2 weeks out]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]
- My contact: [PHONE OR EMAIL]

Requirements:
- Keep it under one page
- Tone: polite, direct, easy to say yes to
- Do not include my full Social Security number or full account number
- State exactly what I want and how to send it

Write only the letter. No extra commentary.
