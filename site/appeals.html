Appeal letters

Use an appeal letter when you want someone to reverse a decision: an insurance denial, a benefits denial, a grade dispute, a parking ticket, or a benefits overpayment notice. Appeals work best when they are calm, factual, and tied to policy.

When to use

  • Health insurance denied a claim or prior authorization
  • Unemployment or disability benefits were denied or reduced
  • A grade or academic decision you disagree with
  • A fine, ticket, or overpayment notice you believe is wrong
Important: education only
This guide is education only, not legal or medical advice. Insurance and benefits appeals can affect your money and your health. If the decision matters a lot, consider asking a patient advocate, benefits counselor, or lawyer to review your letter before you send it.

Step-by-step

1. Gather your facts

  • The decision letter and its date
  • Claim number, case number, or account reference (last 4 digits only if you are unsure about privacy)
  • The deadline to appeal (appeals often have strict deadlines — mark it on your calendar)
  • The policy or rule you think supports your case, if you have it
  • Any supporting documents (doctor's notes, receipts, letters)

2. Use the template

See ../templates/appeal-template.md or templates/printable/printable-appeal.html.

3. Draft with AI

Copy the appeal prompt from AI prompt templates, fill in your details, and paste it into your AI chat tool.

4. Review

Check dates, claim numbers, and deadlines. AI can mix up numbers — verify them yourself. Make sure the tone is calm, not angry.

5. Send before the deadline

Send by certified mail if the decision letter recommends it. Keep copies of everything.

Appeal letter template

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS — optional for email]
[YOUR EMAIL OR PHONE]
[DATE]

[ORGANIZATION NAME]
[APPEALS DEPARTMENT OR ADDRESS]

Re: Appeal of [DECISION TYPE] — [CLAIM/CASE NUMBER]
Date of decision: [DATE]
Deadline to appeal: [DATE]

Dear [APPEALS DEPARTMENT or Contact Name],

I am writing to appeal the [decision type] dated [DATE] regarding
[CLAIM/CASE NUMBER].

The decision states: [briefly summarize the reason given, in your own words].

I disagree for the following reasons:
- [Reason 1 — factual, short]
- [Reason 2 — factual, short]
- [Reason 3 — policy or rule, if you have it]

I have enclosed [list documents: doctor's note, receipt, letter, etc.].

Based on [policy name or rule, if you have it], I request that you [state
exactly what you want: approve the claim, reverse the decision, schedule a
hearing, etc.].

Please respond by [DATE].

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]
[PHONE NUMBER OR EMAIL]

Enclosures: [LIST DOCUMENTS]

AI prompt for appeals

Please write a calm, factual appeal letter. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [ORGANIZATION / APPEALS DEPARTMENT]
- Decision being appealed: [TYPE OF DECISION]
- Date of decision: [DATE]
- Claim or case number: [REFERENCE — last 4 digits only if unsure]
- Reason given for denial: [WHAT THE LETTER SAID]
- Why I disagree: [REASON 1], [REASON 2]
- Policy or rule I rely on: [IF YOU HAVE IT]
- Documents enclosed: [LIST]
- What I want: [APPROVE CLAIM / REVERSE DECISION / HEARING / ETC.]
- Deadline to appeal: [DATE]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]
- My contact: [PHONE OR EMAIL]

Requirements:
- Keep it under one page
- Tone: calm, factual, respectful — not angry or begging
- Do not include insults, threats, or profanity
- Do not include my full Social Security number or full account number
- State clearly what outcome I want and by when

Write only the letter. No extra commentary.

Jamal Wright

jwright@email.com

Blue Sky Health Insurance
Claims Appeals Department

Re: Appeal of claim denial — Claim #X9Y8-Z7W6
Date of decision: March 28, 2026
Deadline to appeal: April 27, 2026

Dear Claims Appeals Department,

I am writing to appeal the denial of claim #X9Y8-Z7W6, dated March 28, 2026,
for my physical therapy visit on March 10, 2026.

The decision states the service was "not medically necessary."

I disagree because:
- My doctor ordered the therapy after my surgery on February 14, 2026.
- The therapy is part of my posted surgical recovery plan.
- My policy covers post-surgical physical therapy.

I have enclosed my doctor's letter of medical necessity and my discharge
instructions.

Based on my policy's post-surgical therapy benefit, I request that you
approve claim #X9Y8-Z7W6.

Please respond by April 20, 2026.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
Jamal Wright
555-010-3040

Enclosures: Doctor's letter of medical necessity, discharge instructions
