AI prompt templates

Copy the prompt for your letter type, paste it into your AI chat tool, fill in the bracketed parts, and send. Always read the AI's draft before you send it.

Complaint letter prompt

Please write a polite but firm complaint letter. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [COMPANY NAME / DEPARTMENT]
- What happened: [DESCRIPTION]
- Date of incident: [DATE]
- Order or account reference: [REFERENCE — last 4 digits only]
- Steps I already took: [WHAT YOU DONE]
- What I want: [REFUND / REPLACEMENT / REPAIR / CORRECTION / APOLOGY]
- Deadline for response: [DATE, about 2 weeks out]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]
- My contact: [PHONE OR EMAIL]

Requirements:
- Keep it under one page
- Tone: calm, specific, respectful
- Do not include insults, threats, or profanity
- Do not include my full Social Security number or full account number
- End with a clear request and a response deadline

Write only the letter. No extra commentary.

Appeal letter prompt

Please write a calm, factual appeal letter. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [ORGANIZATION / APPEALS DEPARTMENT]
- Decision being appealed: [TYPE OF DECISION]
- Date of decision: [DATE]
- Claim or case number: [REFERENCE — last 4 digits only if unsure]
- Reason given for denial: [WHAT THE LETTER SAID]
- Why I disagree: [REASON 1], [REASON 2]
- Policy or rule I rely on: [IF YOU HAVE IT]
- Documents enclosed: [LIST]
- What I want: [APPROVE CLAIM / REVERSE DECISION / HEARING / ETC.]
- Deadline to appeal: [DATE]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]
- My contact: [PHONE OR EMAIL]

Requirements:
- Keep it under one page
- Tone: calm, factual, respectful — not angry or begging
- Do not include insults, threats, or profanity
- Do not include my full Social Security number or full account number
- State clearly what outcome I want and by when

Write only the letter. No extra commentary.

Thank-you note prompt

Please write a short, warm thank-you note. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [NAME]
- What they did: [DESCRIPTION]
- Why it helped or mattered: [ONE SENTENCE]
- Optional extra: [HOW YOU'LL USE THE GIFT / WHEN YOU'LL SEE THEM AGAIN]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]

Requirements:
- 3 to 5 sentences
- Tone: warm, sincere, brief
- No formal business language needed

Write only the note. No extra commentary.

Request letter prompt

Please write a clear, polite request letter. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [ORGANIZATION / DEPARTMENT]
- What I am requesting: [EXACTLY WHAT YOU NEED]
- Details to help them find it: [DATES, NAMES, REFERENCE — last 4 digits only]
- How to send it: [EMAIL OR MAILING ADDRESS]
- Deadline: [DATE, about 2 weeks out]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]
- My contact: [PHONE OR EMAIL]

Requirements:
- Keep it under one page
- Tone: polite, direct, easy to say yes to
- Do not include my full Social Security number or full account number
- State exactly what I want and how to send it

Write only the letter. No extra commentary.

Tips for better results

  1. Be specific in your facts. The more detail you give, the better the draft.
  2. If the first draft isn't right, ask for changes. "Make it shorter," "Make the tone softer," "Add the deadline," etc.
  3. Never paste secrets. No SSNs, full account numbers, passwords, or API keys into an AI chat.
  4. Verify every fact. AI can mix up dates, names, and numbers.
  5. You are the author. AI suggests; you decide and send.
