Writing Letters with AI — Beginner Manual

Welcome. This manual walks you through writing four common letter types with AI: complaints, appeals, thank-you notes, and requests. Each chapter has step-by-step instructions, tone guidance, an AI prompt you can copy and paste, and a printable template.

This guide is published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC and written in plain English — like a friend explaining it to you. No jargon, no condescension.

How to use this manual

  1. Pick the chapter for the letter you need to write.
  2. Read the tone guidance and the "what never to include" section.
  3. Fill in the template with your own details (use placeholders like [YOUR NAME] where you see them).
  4. Copy the AI prompt for that chapter, paste it into your AI chat tool, and fill in the bracketed parts with your details.
  5. Read the AI's draft carefully before you send anything. AI can make mistakes — you are responsible for every word.
  6. Print from templates/printable/ if you need a paper copy.

Chapters

  • Introduction — why use AI for letters, and how to start safely
  • Tone guidance — how your letter should sound
  • What never to include — safety and privacy rules
  • Complaint letters — billing errors, poor service, defective products
  • Appeal letters — insurance denials, benefits decisions, grade disputes
  • Thank-you notes — after appointments, jobs, gifts, favors
  • Request letters — records, accommodations, information, meetings
  • AI prompt templates — copy-and-paste prompts for every letter type

Printable templates

Find print-ready versions in templates/printable/:

  • printable-complaint.html
  • printable-appeal.html
  • printable-thank-you.html
  • printable-request.html

Open any of them in your browser and press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P) to print.

Important reminders

  • This is education, not advice. We do not give medical or legal advice. If your letter affects your health, benefits, insurance, or legal rights, have a qualified professional look it over before you send it.
  • Never include secrets. No Social Security numbers, full account numbers, passwords, API keys, or personal data you wouldn't want shared.
  • You are the author. AI suggests; you decide. Read every draft before sending.
