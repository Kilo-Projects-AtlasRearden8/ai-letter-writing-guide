# Complaint letters

Use a complaint letter when something went wrong: a billing error, poor
service, a defective product, or a missed appointment. The goal is to get it
fixed, not to vent.

## When to use

- You were charged twice or charged the wrong amount
- A product arrived broken or never arrived
- Service was rude, slow, or not as promised
- You want a refund, replacement, repair, or apology

## Step-by-step

### 1. Gather your facts before writing

- Date of the incident or charge
- Order number, account number, or reference number (last 4 digits only if
  you're unsure about privacy)
- Name of the person or department, if you have it
- What you want: refund, replacement, repair, correction, apology

### 2. Use the template

See `templates/complaint-template.md` or `templates/printable/printable-complaint.html`.

### 3. Draft with AI

Copy the complaint prompt from `docs/manual/ai-prompts.md`, fill in your
details, and paste it into your AI chat tool.

### 4. Review

Read the draft. Is it calm? Is it specific? Is it clear what you want? Remove
anything from the "never include" list.

### 5. Send

Email, mail, or use the company's contact form. Keep a copy for yourself.

## Complaint letter template

```text
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS — optional for email]
[YOUR EMAIL OR PHONE]
[DATE]

[COMPANY NAME]
[COMPANY ADDRESS OR DEPARTMENT]

Re: Complaint regarding [ORDER/ACCOUNT NUMBER — last 4 digits only if unsure]

Dear [CONTACT NAME or Customer Service Team],

I am writing about [describe what happened — one or two sentences]. This
occurred on [DATE] involving [ORDER/ACCOUNT REFERENCE].

Specifically:
- [Fact 1 — what happened]
- [Fact 2 — when and where]
- [Fact 3 — any steps you already took]

This caused [describe the impact — money lost, time wasted, inconvenience].

I would like [state exactly what you want: refund, replacement, repair,
correction, apology]. Please respond by [DATE, about 2 weeks out].

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]
[PHONE NUMBER OR EMAIL]
```

## AI prompt for complaints

```
Please write a polite but firm complaint letter. Here are my facts:

- Recipient: [COMPANY NAME / DEPARTMENT]
- What happened: [DESCRIPTION]
- Date of incident: [DATE]
- Order or account reference: [REFERENCE — last 4 digits only]
- Steps I already took: [WHAT YOU DONE]
- What I want: [REFUND / REPLACEMENT / REPAIR / CORRECTION / APOLOGY]
- Deadline for response: [DATE, about 2 weeks out]
- My name: [YOUR NAME]
- My contact: [PHONE OR EMAIL]

Requirements:
- Keep it under one page
- Tone: calm, specific, respectful
- Do not include insults, threats, or profanity
- Do not include my full Social Security number or full account number
- End with a clear request and a response deadline

Write only the letter. No extra commentary.
```

```
Customer Service Department

I would like a replacement pitcher or a full refund of $49.99. Please
respond by April 24.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
Maria Lopez
555-010-2030
```
