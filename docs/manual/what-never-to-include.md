# What never to include

AI makes letter writing easier, but some things should never go in a letter —
whether you wrote it yourself or AI helped. This is your safety checklist.
Check every draft against this list before sending.

## Personal data you should protect

Never include:

- **Social Security number** (full or partial)
- **Full bank account or credit card numbers** (use only the last 4 digits if needed)
- **Passwords, PINs, or API keys**
- **Date of birth** (unless the recipient absolutely requires it, such as a
  medical-records request — then include only what's needed)
- **Home address** in email letters (fine for printed mail, but not email)
- **Medical details** beyond what is necessary for the request
- **Anyone else's personal data** (family members, coworkers, etc.)

Rule of thumb: if you wouldn't want it posted on a bulletin board, don't put
it in the letter.

## Never include when writing with AI

- **Anything you haven't read.** AI can invent facts, dates, and names.
  Verify every detail in the draft.
- **Placeholder text you forgot to fill in.** `[YOUR NAME]` and `[DATE]` look
  unprofessional and can cause real problems.
- **Legal conclusions.** Don't write "this is negligence" or "you broke the
  law." Describe what happened and let professionals draw conclusions.
- **Medical diagnoses or treatment advice.** You are not a doctor (unless you
  are, writing professionally). Describe symptoms or events only.
- **Threats, insults, or profanity.** They undermine your case and can be
  used against you.
- **False or exaggerated claims.** Stick to what actually happened.

## Healthcare context (education only)

If you are a healthcare worker writing about work matters, remember:

- Do not include patient names or identifying details in letters to AI tools.
- This guide is education only — it is never medical advice.
- Follow your employer's privacy policies (such as HIPAA in the U.S.) before
  sharing anything about patient care.

## Before you send: the 30-second check

1. Did I include my full name and contact info?
2. Did I remove SSNs, account numbers, and passwords?
3. Did I read every sentence myself?
4. Did I remove all placeholder brackets?
5. Is the tone calm and respectful?
6. Is it clear what I want?

If any answer is no, fix it before sending.
