# Writing Letters with AI — Beginner Manual

Welcome. This manual walks you through writing four common letter types with
AI: **complaints, appeals, thank-you notes, and requests**. Each chapter has
step-by-step instructions, tone guidance, an AI prompt you can copy and paste,
and a printable template.

This guide is published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** and written in plain
English — like a friend explaining it to you. No jargon, no condescension.

## How to use this manual

1. Pick the chapter for the letter you need to write.
2. Read the tone guidance and the "what never to include" section.
3. Fill in the template with your own details (use placeholders like
   `[YOUR NAME]` where you see them).
4. Copy the AI prompt for that chapter, paste it into your AI chat tool,
   and fill in the bracketed parts with your details.
5. Read the AI's draft carefully before you send anything. AI can make
   mistakes — you are responsible for every word.
6. Print from `templates/printable/` if you need a paper copy.

## Important reminders

- **This is education, not advice.** We do not give medical or legal advice.
  If your letter affects your health, benefits, insurance, or legal rights,
  have a qualified professional look it over before you send it.
- **Never include secrets.** No Social Security numbers, full account numbers,
  passwords, API keys, or personal data you wouldn't want shared.
- **You are the author.** AI suggests; you decide. Read every draft before
  sending.
