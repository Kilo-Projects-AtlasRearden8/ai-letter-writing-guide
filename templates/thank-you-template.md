# Thank-you note template (fill-in)

Copy this template, replace every `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER]` with your details,
and paste it into your AI chat tool along with the thank-you prompt from
`docs/manual/ai-prompts.md` — or just fill it in yourself; thank-you notes are
short enough to write by hand.

---

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS OR EMAIL]
[DATE]

Dear [NAME],

Thank you for [what they did]. [One sentence about why it helped or mattered
to you.]

[Optional: one more sentence — how you'll use the gift, what happens next,
or when you hope to see them again.]

With thanks,
[YOUR NAME]
