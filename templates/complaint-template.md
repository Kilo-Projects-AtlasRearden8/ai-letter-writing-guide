# Complaint letter template (fill-in)

Copy this template, replace every `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER]` with your details,
and paste it into your AI chat tool along with the complaint prompt from
`docs/manual/ai-prompts.md`.

---

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS — optional for email]
[YOUR EMAIL OR PHONE]
[DATE]

[COMPANY NAME]
[COMPANY ADDRESS OR DEPARTMENT]

Re: Complaint regarding [ORDER/ACCOUNT NUMBER — last 4 digits only if unsure]

Dear [CONTACT NAME or Customer Service Team],

I am writing about [describe what happened — one or two sentences]. This
occurred on [DATE] involving [ORDER/ACCOUNT REFERENCE].

Specifically:
- [Fact 1 — what happened]
- [Fact 2 — when and where]
- [Fact 3 — any steps you already took]

This caused [describe the impact — money lost, time wasted, inconvenience].

I would like [state exactly what you want: refund, replacement, repair,
correction, apology]. Please respond by [DATE, about 2 weeks out].

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]
[PHONE NUMBER OR EMAIL]
